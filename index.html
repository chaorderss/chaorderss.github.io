cinema-uploader

A private publishing tool for the owner's own YouTube channels.

cinema-uploader is a single-operator tool. It publishes videos produced by its owner to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos with their title, description, tags and chapters.
  • Sets thumbnails and adds subtitle tracks.
  • Reads back upload and processing status, and removes test uploads.

Who can use it

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and it only acts on channels whose owner has authorized it through Google's consent screen. Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions.
