Privacy Policy

cinema-uploader · Effective October 5, 2026

Overview

cinema-uploader is a private tool run by a single individual (the “operator”) to publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own YouTube channels. It uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

When a channel owner authorizes cinema-uploader through Google's consent screen, it receives OAuth tokens for the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl. With them it:

  • reads the authorized channel's ID and title, to know which channel a token belongs to;
  • uploads videos and sets their title, description, tags, privacy status and thumbnail;
  • adds or updates subtitle tracks on those videos;
  • reads the upload and processing status of videos it uploaded, and deletes its own test uploads.

It does not read comments, subscribers, watch history, email, contacts or any other Google data.

How we use it

Only to publish and manage the operator's own videos on the authorized channel. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and channel IDs are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator's user account. There is no server, database or cloud copy. Tokens are sent only to Google's APIs, over HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Data goes only to Google and YouTube, as needed to call their APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

cinema-uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions or the Google security settings page; a revoked token stops working immediately and the operator deletes the stored copy. Videos already published stay on YouTube under YouTube's own policies until you delete them there.
